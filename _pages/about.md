I am a Robotics Systems Engineer passionate about building intelligent robotic systems. My work spans reinforcement learning for locomotion, SLAM, and vision-based navigation using ROS2. I currently work at Parth Infotech Ltd., and my journey includes contributions to defense robotics through a DRDO internship and experience in cloud infrastructure and data systems at TCS.

My academic path includes an MTech in Robotics & AI from COEP Technological University, Pune, where I engaged in actuator simulation research, and a BE in Electrical Engineering. I’ve earned certifications in Google Cloud and data analytics, and my research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, control systems, and robotics embodiment.

A data-driven roboticist with real-world system deployment experience
======

My career combines software reliability in cloud systems with cutting-edge AI for robotics. I bring deep knowledge in Python, SQL, ROS2, and Google Cloud Platform, and I've deployed learning-based controllers both in simulation (Isaac Sim) and on real hardware.

Notable technical strengths include:

- Deploying RL-based locomotion controllers  
- Simulation & control design for actuators  
- Optimizing DAGs and SQL pipelines in Airflow/BigQuery  
- Creating reproducible research pipelines using Python and cloud tools
